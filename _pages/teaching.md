I have taught in two quite different settings: one-to-one physics tuition in the UK and scuba instruction as a PADI and BSAC Open Water Instructor. Both have taught me to begin with what a student already understands, explain the reasoning behind a task, and use feedback to see whether an explanation has worked.

## Teaching experience

### Physics tutoring

I have provided one-to-one physics tuition in the UK, helping students work through quantitative problems and understand the underlying concepts. Working individually has allowed me to adapt the pace and explanation to each student's needs.

### Scuba instruction

As a PADI and BSAC Open Water Instructor, I have taught practical diving skills and the safety procedures behind them. Students need to understand why a procedure matters and be able to carry it out reliably. I assess their progress, give specific feedback, and adjust instruction as needed.

## Teaching interests

I am interested in teaching introductory economics, applied microeconomics, environmental and natural resource economics, energy economics, and quantitative methods.
